Customer Service Digital Employee — GDP Labs

Jul 2026 – Sep 2026
InternshipAgentic AIIntegrationsLLM Optimisation

Software developer internship building features for a Customer Service Digital Employee (CS-DE) — an agent that handles support work through conversation. My work covered:

  • Connecting the CS-DE to a third-party board to fetch board data for specified regions.
  • Creating and updating tickets on that board directly from a chat conversation.
  • Reading existing tickets and generating PDF and DOCX drafts of them from chat.
  • Token optimisation — profiling how many tokens each conversation consumed and cutting the cost per interaction.
  • Model migration — evaluating candidate language models for the agent and re-verifying the core conversational flows on each one, including adversarial testing for cases where an agent reports success on work it never actually performed.

Efficient Chess Engine — Kaggle × Google

Dec 2024 – Feb 2025
C++OptimisationTop 2%

The FIDE & Google Efficient Chess AI Challenge asked for a chess engine running under severe constraints: 5 MiB of RAM, a single 2.20 GHz core, and a 64 KB submission size limit. I studied and benchmarked a range of open-source engines, then optimised one to fit inside that budget without losing playing strength.

Finished 18th out of 1128 teams — the top 2% of the leaderboard.
